# Glossary

Diary
: One person's ordered chain of trips and activities during the observation window.

Trip
: A movement from an origin to a destination, with a purpose, mode, and timing information.

Episode
: A continuous interval where the person is either doing an activity or travelling by one mode.

State sequence
: A fixed-length sequence of labels created by slicing the day into equal time bins and assigning each bin to an activity or travel state.

Transition rate
: The empirical probability of moving from one state to another in observed sequences.

Substitution cost
: The optimal-matching cost of replacing one state with another.

Indel
: The insertion or deletion cost used by optimal matching when aligning sequences of different shapes.

Dissimilarity matrix
: A square table of pairwise sequence differences. The diagonal is zero because each sequence is identical to itself.

Average linkage
: A hierarchical clustering method where the distance between two clusters is the average pairwise dissimilarity between their members.
